**Retrieval:**
In a multi-document RAG system, retrieval is done by first ranking the relevance of individual documents by calculating their similarity to the query using various metrics such as term frequency-inverse document frequency (TF-IDF), Cosine similarity, or other relevance scoring algorithms. **Generation:**
Context-aware answer generation in a multi-document RAG system involves leveraging the retrieved documents to generate coherent and concise answers. This is achieved by:
1. **Incorporating document features**: The generation model is trained to incorporate features from the retrieved documents, such as summary statistics, key phrases, and entity mentions.
2. **Contextualized understanding**: The model is designed to understand the context of the query by incorporating knowledge from the retrieved documents, enabling the generation of answers that are relevant to the query.
3. **Textualization**: The generation model is trained to produce human-readable answers that accurately convey the context and requirements of the query, often utilizing techniques such as language modeling, sequence generation, or neural text generation algorithms.
